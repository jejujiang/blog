title: "embrace all that comes your way"
---

money lostt can be earned again
a job lost can be found again
lost keys can be replaced
life is simply about meeting
each challenge as it comes
no need to wrestle with yourself
whether you feared it or not what happened has happened
there is no need to dread the unknow
or escape reality anymore
every experience
be a joy or sorrow
is part of the journey
when you look upon the world with peace
the world will gently heal you in return
embrace all that comes your way
and welcome a happier more liberated version of yourself.


these are the three sentences children long to hear the most
have you ever said them?
the first one it's all right I'm here for you
when children run into difficulties
fell wronged or break down emotionally
these words bring them immense a sense of security
they will know that no matter what happens
parents will always be their strongest support
second one you have done really well
do not only focus on your children's flaws and shortcomings
learn to appreciate their efforts and progress
this sentence can greatly 
boost their self confidence
and let them realize that they deserve recognition
even if they are imperfect
the third one I will always love you
this love has nothings to do with grades
preformance or obedience
help children understand that
parental love is unconditional
and they are worthy of love no matter who they are
may we all support our children and help
them grow into the adults they want to be.
