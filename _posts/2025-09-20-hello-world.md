**Hello World**

In 1972, a revolution quietly began at Bell Labs. Dennis Ritchie's C language emerged from the shadows of assembly, its elegance woven with pointers and structures. When the first "hello world" pulsed through a terminal, it wasn't just text—it was the founding syntax of the digital age. This simple greeting became the universal key, unlocking everything from operating systems to spacecraft, building the very world we now inhabit.

**你好，世界**

1972年，一场革命在贝尔实验室悄然开启。丹尼斯·里奇创造的C语言挣脱了汇编语言的束缚，用指针和结构体编织出优雅的代码。当第一个"hello world"在终端闪烁时，它不仅是文字——更是数字时代的奠基宣言。这声简单的问候化作万能钥匙，开启了从操作系统到宇宙飞船的一切，构筑了我们如今栖居的数码世界。
